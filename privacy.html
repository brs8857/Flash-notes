Your privacy in Flash Notes

Flash Notes is a notes app for students. This page says, in plain English, what we know about you, why, and what you can do about it.

The short version

  • Your notes are private. Only you can open them. Nobody else who uses Flash Notes can see them, and there is no way to make a note public or share it.
  • We only get your name and email address from Google, to make your account. Nothing else: not your photo, your contacts, your files or your location.
  • No ads. No tracking. No selling data. There are no analytics or advertising tools in this app at all.
  • You can download all your notes or delete your account and everything in it at any time, in Settings.

Where your notes are kept

  • On your device, inside your browser’s storage, so the app works without internet. Signing out removes them from the device.
  • In your account, in a database run for us by Supabase (see below), in [FILL IN: Supabase region, for example “London, United Kingdom (eu-west-2)”]. The database is set up so that each row of notes can only be read or changed by the account that owns it.

Your notes are protected in transit (HTTPS) and stored encrypted on Supabase’s disks. They are not end-to-end encrypted, which means the people who run this service could technically open the database. We do not read your notes, and would only access the database to fix a fault, keep the service secure, or if the law required it.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can:

  • See and take a copy of your information. Settings → “Download my notes” gives you everything as one file, at any time. “Export as Markdown” gives you your text as a simple document.
  • Delete it. Settings → “Delete my account” erases your account and all your notes, permanently.
  • Correct it. Your name and email come from your Google account; change them there. You can edit your notes at any time.
  • Object, or ask us to limit what we do with your information, or ask any question about it, using the contact details above.

We will answer within one month. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or 0303 123 1113.

If you are under 18

Flash Notes is made for students, so it is built to be safe for young people by default:

  • Everything is private by default, and there is nothing to switch on that would make it less private. There are no public profiles, no sharing, no messaging, no friends lists and no comments.
  • We collect the smallest amount of information that makes the app work (see the table above).
  • There is no advertising, no profiling, no tracking and no location data, and nothing in the app nudges you to give more information or to weaken your privacy.
  • You can see, download and delete your own information yourself, without having to ask anyone.
  • This page is written to be understood without legal knowledge. If anything is unclear, ask a parent, carer or teacher, or contact us.

Cookies and similar storage

Flash Notes does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It stores what it strictly needs in your browser: your notes and settings, the app’s files so it works offline, and a sign-in token that keeps you signed in. Signing out removes the token and your notes from the device.
